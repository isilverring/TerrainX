# Challenge coverage

TerrainX addresses the Earth-analog challenge through a reproducible discovery and experiment-planning workflow.

| Dimension | Implemented workflow | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Discover terrestrial analog leads | Original study and separate expanded 130-cell search with four benchmarks | Search is limited to declared windows; candidates are preliminary |
| Compare Moon and Mars conditions | Separate F/U/C, configurable mission model, terrain comparison and transfer physics | Scores are model assumptions, not universal similarity or probability |
| Explain evidence and uncertainty | Source Inspector, literature cases, missing-evidence sensitivity and Passport | Mapped context and published measurements retain their geographic scope |
| Prepare experiments | Candidate-specific field stations, Research Lab protocols, JSON/GPX/print/share | Proposals do not imply access, sampling or safety approval |
| Investigate lunar-base analogs | Eight feature-centre LOLA/Diviner references, bounded illumination and autonomy scenarios | Historical feature names are not current landing polygons; resources and utility remain unassessed |
| Present reproducible findings | Guided demo, pitch, pinned research snapshots and offline preparation | External maps and citations require internet |
