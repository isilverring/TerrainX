Production mark: `frontend/public/brand/terrainx-mark.svg`. The broad angular X and two tapered diagonal orbital arcs use cool silver `#C6D7E8` and cyan `#35C3B0` on a transparent background.

The vector trace follows the original raster mark in `logo-original.jpg`, including its detached upper-left segment, rising arm, separated lower-right pieces and orbital spacing. The source raster is retained for brand reference. The mark is decorative branding, separate from scientific imagery.
